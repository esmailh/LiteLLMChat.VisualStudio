# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

This project welcomes contributors regardless of experience, background, identity, or the language
they use. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the code, not the person.
- Use English or Persian in issues and pull requests; either is fine.
- Keep discussion on topic and free of confidential material.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory comments.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions, or reviews.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the maintainer through GitHub — a private message to [@esmailh](https://github.com/esmailh),
or the private reporting flow in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) when confidentiality matters.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban a
participant. Decisions are made in good faith and are final.
